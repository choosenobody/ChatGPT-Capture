# Case 016 — Cross-company evergreen (channel economics)

Why this case matters
---------------------
Three seemingly unrelated businesses share a channel-economics
mechanism. The right artefact is one *cross-context* evergreen
("long-channel businesses need to separate channel margin from
end-consumer margin") — not three single-company notes.

Failure modes
-------------
- Three single-name notes created (one per company). (compression
  miss; the cross-context signal is lost)
- The evergreen captures only the Moutai case. (recall miss for
  the MIXUE/telecom evidence)
- The evergreen is collapsed with case 008's channel-migration
  evergreen. (dedup miss — these are related but distinct
  principles)
