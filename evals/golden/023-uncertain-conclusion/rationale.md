# Case 023 — Uncertain conclusion (demand weakness vs channel destocking)

Why this case matters
---------------------
The user explicitly admits they can't tell. The right artefact
is an open question with the *uncertainty* preserved, not a
forced conclusion. Saving "it was demand weakness" without
evidence is the canonical epistemic error.

Failure modes
-------------
- A confident "demand weakness" conclusion is recorded. (epistemic
  miss)
- The note is not tagged as `type: question`. (recall miss on the
  open-question artefact)
- The note is collapsed with case 008's channel-migration evergreen.
  (dedup miss; this is an unresolved case, 008 is a settled
  principle)
