# High RONIC reduces reinvestment required for growth

## Mechanism
Incremental ROIC = ΔNOPAT / ΔInvested Capital. High incremental ROIC means each new dollar of growth needs less new capital.

## Implication
Growth × RONIC, not growth alone, determines whether growth creates value.

## Failure conditions
1. Metric is incremental, not average.
2. Accounting distortions can inflate temporarily.
3. Competitive entry compresses over time.
