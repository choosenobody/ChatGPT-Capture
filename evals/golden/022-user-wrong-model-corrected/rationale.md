# Case 022 — User's wrong model corrected

Why this case matters
---------------------
This is the "model correction" case. The user's prior belief
("revenue growth → shareholder value") is structurally wrong.
The librarian must REVERSE it, preserve the history, and create
(or update) the canonical "incremental economics" evergreen.

Failure modes
-------------
- The prior belief is silently overwritten. (HARD-FAIL: silent
  overwrite)
- Direction is `weakened` instead of `reversed`. (belief direction
  miss)
- The evergreen is missing the *implication* that revenue growth
  alone can destroy value. (evergreen quality partial)
