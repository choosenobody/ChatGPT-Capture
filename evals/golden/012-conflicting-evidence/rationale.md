# Case 012 — Hypothesis ≠ Fact

Why this case matters
---------------------
The canonical epistemic-discipline failure mode: a confident-sounding
forward-looking statement is actually a HYPOTHESIS. The user's exact
question is "is this a fact?" — the answer must be no.

Failure modes
-------------
- Saved as a fact ("Telcos will raise prices 5-10% next year").
  (HARD-FAIL: hypothesis promoted to fact)
- Promoted to an evergreen. (HARD-FAIL)
- Saved without `epistemic_kind: HYPOTHESIS`. (epistemic miss)
