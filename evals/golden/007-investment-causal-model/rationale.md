# Case 007 — Investment causal model: revenue growth ≠ value creation

Why this case matters
---------------------
The reusable principle is *cross-domain*: it applies to growth-stage
tech, mature industrials, even personal-finance decisions about
reinvesting dividends. If the evergreen is captured as a generic
"growth investing tip" it loses cross-context power.

Failure modes
-------------
- Evergreen captured as a quote rather than a mental model.
- Evergreen collapsed with case 006 evergreen. (dedup miss; the
  librarian should detect title-similarity but concept-distinct)
- The note forgets the *causal* direction — "growth does not imply
  value creation", not the reverse.
