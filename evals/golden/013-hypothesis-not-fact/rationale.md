# Case 013 — Hypothesis not fact (twin of 012 but different shape)

Why this case matters
---------------------
The user explicitly says "they contradict" — the right behaviour
is to *record* the contradiction, not collapse it. Either save
two notes (Claim A + Claim B) or one note with both claims and a
resolution-evidence section.

Failure modes
-------------
- Only one of Claim A or Claim B is preserved. (recall miss)
- The contradiction is presented as resolved. (epistemic miss)
- The note is treated as fact. (epistemic miss; `must_not_promote_to_fact`)
