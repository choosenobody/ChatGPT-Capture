# Channel migration ≠ end-demand weakness

## One-line conclusion
Distributor inventory changes are not the same as end-demand weakness.

## Previous understanding
Moutai wholesale softness = demand collapse.

## New evidence / reasoning
Distributor -> direct -> DTC reclassification moves where revenue is recognised without changing consumption.

## Updated understanding
Channel migration and end-demand weakness are distinct phenomena with different evidence: scan data vs accounting reclassification.

## Why it matters
Mistaking one for the other leads to wrong investment decisions.

## Generalizable principle
Always separate *where* revenue is recognised from *whether* demand exists.
