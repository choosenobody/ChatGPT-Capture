# Case 014 — Open question (Lenovo AI server SSG attach rate)

Why this case matters
---------------------
Many conversations end in unresolved questions. The right artefact
is a `type: question` learning, with `why-it-matters` and
`resolution evidence`. It must not be promoted to a fact or to an
evergreen.

Failure modes
-------------
- A guess at the attach rate is recorded as fact. (epistemic miss)
- The question is dropped on the floor entirely. (recall miss)
- The question is linked to an evergreen about AI inference
  economics without the resolution-evidence field. (linking miss)
