# Case 006 — New mental model: Growth × RONIC

Why this case matters
---------------------
This is the canonical "new evergreen" case. The conversation yields
exactly one reusable principle: a high incremental RONIC lowers the
capital required to fund growth, and growth × RONIC — not growth
alone — is what determines whether growth creates value.

Failure modes
-------------
- The extractor writes 3+ inbox notes (one per company, one for the
  formula, one for the conclusion). (dedup / compression miss)
- The evergreen captures the formula but not the *implication* or
  *failure conditions*. (Evergreen quality partial)
- The evergreen is missing the cross-context test: this should be
  applicable to non-investment contexts too. (Evergreen quality miss)
